Togal.AI Demo

This project focused on evaluating the native AI capabilities of Togal.AI while also gaining experience with its manual takeoff tools. Five different plan sets were analyzed, each varying in level of detail and type (elevations, foundations, and floor plans). The goal was to assess accuracy and identify strengths and weaknesses of the system.

The most common errors occurred in identifying wall perimeters and centerlines. The AI frequently detected elements that resembled walls but were not, and often misaligned centerlines along interior or exterior dimensions. Conversely, the system performed well in identifying repeated objects such as doors, toilets, and showers.

Another limitation observed was the inability to generate quantities from pure elevation drawings, which often resulted in errors after extended processing times.

Estimating with AI

This project explored the use of AI tools such as ChatGPT and Claude for quantity takeoffs and cost estimation. Various prompts and configurations were tested to determine which methods produced the most accurate results.

Key findings included the importance of providing clear and specific inputs, as well as the impact of using advanced “thinking” modes, which improved accuracy but increased response time. Higher-tier AI models also produced more detailed and refined outputs.

Additional insights showed that Claude tended to be more detail-oriented, while ChatGPT had limitations such as not being able to directly generate Word documents. The quality of scanned drawings was also found to significantly impact output accuracy.
