About Me

I am Elias Hord, a UNCC graduate student in CMET 6000-080: AI in Construction & Facilities Management. I have an undergraduate degree in civil engineering and have worked for NCDOT as an engineer for the past two years. In this course we have explored the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Construction Management, and Facilities Management. Throughout this semester, I have analyzed emerging AI tools, evaluated industry applications, and explored automation strategies in project delivery and building operations. Please enjoy looking through my work.
